Title: Troubleshooting Wi-Fi Signal Attenuation, Co-Channel Interference, and Dead Zones
Section: IEEE 802.11 Wireless Optimization Guidelines
Category: wifi_signal_issue
Source: IEEE / Network Engineering Guidelines

Summary:
Wi-Fi signal propagation is bounded by free-space path loss and RF attenuation when penetrating structural elements (concrete, bricks, tinted glass, metal lathe). 
Key guidelines:
1. 2.4GHz band (channels 1, 6, 11 non-overlapping) provides greater wall penetration but lower maximum bandwidth (up to ~100 Mbps practical).
2. 5GHz band (channels 36-165) provides high throughput and wider channel bonding (40/80 MHz) but attenuates rapidly beyond 1-2 standard walls.
3. Repositioning the router to a central, elevated position (at least 3-5 feet above floor level) away from metallic appliances and microwaves dramatically improves RSSI signal-to-noise ratio.
4. If clients experience roaming ping-pong between 2.4G and 5G, separate the SSID names (e.g. HomeNet_2.4G and HomeNet_5G) to anchor the client.
